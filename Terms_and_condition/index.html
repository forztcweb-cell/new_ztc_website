1INTRODUCTION#

1.1Purpose#

1.1.1These Terms and Conditions (the Terms) set out the legal framework under which Zatics Intelligence supplies Services, Software and Systems to its business clients, and the rights and responsibilities of each party in that relationship.

1.1.2The Terms are intended to make the commercial relationship clear before work begins, to define how Scope, Fees, timelines, ownership, data and risk are managed, and to provide an orderly process for changes, approvals, payment and dispute resolution.

1.1.3The Services are supplied to organizations for business purposes. They are not supplied to individuals acting as consumers.

1.2Acceptance of Terms#

1.2.1The Client accepts these Terms by doing any of the following:

(a) signing an Agreement that incorporates or refers to these Terms;

(b) approving a Proposal, Blueprint or Change Order through the Client Portal, by email or by another electronic means, where that document refers to these Terms;

(c) issuing a purchase order that Zatics Intelligence accepts in writing; or

(d) authorizing the commencement of Services or accessing or using any Software, System or Client Portal supplied by Zatics Intelligence.

1.2.2The individual who accepts these Terms on behalf of the Client confirms that he or she has authority to bind the Client.

1.2.3Terms contained in a purchase order, vendor onboarding form, procurement policy or other document issued by the Client do not apply to the Services unless Zatics Intelligence expressly accepts them in a written document signed by an authorized representative of Zatics Intelligence.

1.3When These Terms Become Binding#

1.3.1These Terms become binding on the Client and Zatics Intelligence on the earliest of the events described in the preceding Section 1.2.

1.3.2A discussion, presentation, estimate or Proposal does not by itself create an obligation on Zatics Intelligence to perform a Project or on the Client to purchase Services. Zatics Intelligence is not required to begin a Project until an Agreement has been executed, the Blueprint has been approved where applicable, and any Upfront Fee that the Agreement makes a condition of commencement has been received.

1.3.3A Proposal remains open for acceptance for the period stated in it or, if no period is stated, for thirty (30) calendar days from its date of issue.

1.4Relationship Between These Terms and Individual Agreements#

1.4.1These Terms form the general framework for the relationship. Each engagement is documented in an Agreement that records the commercial particulars of that engagement, including the identity of the Client, the Fees, the Subscription Term and the Scope.

1.4.2Where the Client has more than one Project or License, these Terms apply to each of them separately. An Agreement may take the form of a master agreement supported by separate order forms, statements of work or license schedules.

1.4.3An Agreement may modify a provision of these Terms only if it identifies the Section being modified and states the modification expressly. A general statement that the Agreement prevails does not displace any Section of these Terms that is not identified.

1.5Effective Date#

1.5.1These Terms take effect on the Terms Effective Date. As between Zatics Intelligence and a particular Client, the version of these Terms in force on the Effective Date of the Agreement governs that Agreement for its duration, subject to Section 1.6.

1.6Updates to These Terms#

1.6.1Zatics Intelligence may update these Terms from time to time. An updated version will be published on the Client Portal or the official website, and Zatics Intelligence will notify the Client by email at least thirty (30) days before the updated version takes effect.

1.6.2An updated version applies to Agreements entered into on or after its effective date and to any renewal of a Subscription Term that begins on or after that date. It does not apply to a Project or Subscription Term already in progress under an existing Agreement unless:

(a) the Client agrees to it in writing;

(b) the change is required by Applicable Law, an order of a competent authority, or a mandatory requirement of a Third Party Service provider;

(c) the change is necessary to address a security risk; or

(d) the change does not materially reduce the Client's rights or materially increase the Client's obligations.

1.6.3Zatics Intelligence will maintain a record of each version in Section 39 and will provide earlier versions to the Client on request.

(j) consulting, being technical analysis, assessment and advice on systems, architecture and automation; and

(k) support and maintenance, being the operation, correction and updating of Systems after delivery.
